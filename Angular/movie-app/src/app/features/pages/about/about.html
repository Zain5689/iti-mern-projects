About MovieApp

MovieApp is a dedicated platform for cinema enthusiasts. Our mission is to provide a seamless experience for discovering trending movies, exploring cast details, and staying updated with the latest releases in the film industry.

"Our goal is to bring the world of cinema closer to your fingertips with a fast, clean, and modern interface."

The Technology

Built using **Angular 18**, **Tailwind CSS**, and powered by the **TMDB API**, this project demonstrates modern frontend practices including modular architecture, standalone components, and responsive design.
